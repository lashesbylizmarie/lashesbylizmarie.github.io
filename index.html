preparing for your lash appointment

Do's:

  • Remove all makeup prior to coming to your appointment
  • Please be on time
  • Wear comfortable clothing
  • Remove contacts
  • Use the bathroom before your appointment starts

Don'ts:

  • Apply face creams or sunblock around the eyes
  • Wear mascara and/or any makeup
  • Drink coffee or energy drinks

caring for your lash extensions

  • sleep on your back or side

    This helps avoid pressure on your lashes, preventing them from getting crushed.

  • clean & brush daily

    Use a lash-friendly cleanser to keep your lashes clean and free from debris.

    Use a clean spoolie brush to gently comb through your lashes.

  • avoid using mascara

    It’s tough to remove and can damage both extensions and natural lashes.

  • avoid oil based products

    oils can break down the adhesive that bonds the extensions to your natural lashes, causing them to fall out prematurely.

  • avoid eyelash curlers

    Mechanical lash curlers can crimp and break your lash extensions.

  • avoid pulling or rubbing eyes

    Pulling out the lash extension can lead to pulling out the natural lash attached to it causing an uneven lash line and a compromised lash growth cycle.

    Rubbing can cause lash extensions to fall out and irritate your eyes.

  • avoid steam & sauna

    Excessive heat and moisture can weaken the lash adhesive.

  • schedule regular fills

    Keep up with your lash extension appointments to maintain fullness and shape.
