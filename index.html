meet your lash artist

Welcome! My name is Liz. I’m a licensed cosmetologist of 6 years and an experienced lash artist of 2 years.

I give all of my focus to providing my clients with perfect lash sets by measuring out eye shapes/faces to accurately choose the best lash style for you!

In my home salon, I stride myself on providing only the highest quality lash services and customer experience. I look forward to meeting you very soon!

first time booking?

To book your appointment please dm on instagram @lizmarie.lashes

Please leave your name, preferred method of contact and the lash style you would like to book for.

If you have any questions or appointment time requests feel free to message me.

Please read through the following policies and select the service you’d like to receive.

Please make sure you arrive 5 minutes prior to your first appointment to ask any questions and get an idea of what you’re wanting to have done.

Please note: Lash extensions are considered a luxury service and can take anywhere from 2-4 hours for a full set.

booking policies

Cancellations:
Rescheduling or cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to your appointment.

If you are feeling unwell, please reschedule. This includes runny nose, cough, eye infection, etc.

Late/No Show:
If you are late to your appointment by 15 minutes your appointment will be rescheduled.

No text no show for a second time will no longer be able to book an appointment with lizmarie.lashes

Guests:
Guests are limited, please let us know before your appointment if a guest will accompany you at the time of your service.

booking policies

Payment Policy:
Acceptable forms of payment: cash, zelle, cashapp, or apple cash.

No refunds offered, if there are any issues within the first 72 hours a free touch up or removal will be offered. This only applies if correct aftercare has been followed.

Lash Infills:
At least 50% of lash extensions have to be present to be considered a fill in. Please keep this in mind when booking a lash fill.

Foreign lash fills will not be accepted. If wanting to book for a lash appointment, a removal and a new full set will need to be completed.
